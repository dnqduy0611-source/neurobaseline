nNeuroBaseline

Protecting brain health earlier by comparing each person with themselves.

Short cognitive tasks on a phone, a personal baseline, and a clear rule for when a change deserves a clinician's attention.

Research prototype. NeuroBaseline is a screening and monitoring tool. It makes no diagnosis and is not a medical device. The example profile and the clinician's cohort are simulated; no real person's data are shown.
